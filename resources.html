THE TOOLKIT

Six practical power-ups for your next application. Open a card, take what you need and get ready to play.

01YOUR CV, SORTED+

Keep your CV clear and targeted. Put your strongest relevant skills near the top, use readable headings and check dates, contact details and spelling before submitting.

  • Lead with skills relevant to the opportunity.
  • Use short bullet points and concrete results.
  • Save a clean PDF with your name in the filename.
02OWN THE INTERVIEW+

Prepare short examples of your work using a situation, action and result structure. Research the organisation and prepare questions about the role.

  • Practise answers out loud, not only in your head.
  • Bring one example that shows how you solved a problem.
  • Prepare two thoughtful questions to ask at the end.
03BUILD YOUR PROFILE+

Use a professional profile to show your skills, projects, education and experience. Keep the same core facts across your CV and profile.

  • Choose a clear, current profile photo.
  • Describe what you can do, not only what you have studied.
  • Add projects, volunteering or community work.
04WRITE A COVER LETTER THAT WORKS+

Explain why the specific role fits your skills. Avoid repeating your entire CV. Mention relevant evidence and keep it concise.

  • Name the role and organisation directly.
  • Connect one or two strengths to the opportunity.
  • Finish with a clear, professional call to action.
05SPOT THE SCAM+

Be suspicious of payment requests, guaranteed jobs, pressure to act immediately, requests for passwords or OTPs, and links that do not match the real organisation. Verify independently.

  • Never pay to apply or to secure a job.
  • Check the sender and destination URL carefully.
  • Contact the organisation through its official website.
06GET YOUR DOCS READY+

Prepare a clean folder containing your CV, qualification records and other commonly requested documents. Share only what the application genuinely requires.

  • Keep scans readable and use descriptive filenames.
  • Check whether documents need certification.
  • Remove sensitive information that is not requested.
